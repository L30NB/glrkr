Who we are

NJB Capital is an investment and asset management firm working at the intersection of origination, structuring and active management.

We bring investors and opportunities together with care and discretion. Our approach is deliberately selective: we take on fewer opportunities and give each the attention it requires, from first assessment to final realisation.

Investment decisions are guided by judgement, patience and a clear view of risk, and are made with the interests of our investors and partners in mind.

Club Deals

Origination and management of tailored investment opportunities for selected investors.

The Club Deal model

A Club Deal brings together a small group of selected investors to take part in a single investment opportunity.

NJB Capital originates the opportunity, structures the transaction and manages the investment on behalf of the group. Investors participate alongside one another, with interests that are clearly defined and aligned from the outset.

From first look to realisation

  1. 01

    Origination

    We identify and source opportunities through our network and our own research, and assess them against clear criteria before any investor is approached.

  2. 02

    Structuring

    Each transaction is structured individually, with attention to legal, tax and governance considerations and to the alignment of all parties.

  3. 03

    Investor Selection

    Opportunities are shared with a limited circle of investors whose objectives, horizon and expectations fit the transaction.

  4. 04

    Execution

    We coordinate due diligence, documentation and closing, keeping the process orderly and transparent for every participant.

  5. 05

    Active Management

    After closing, we monitor performance, support the underlying business or asset and keep investors informed.

  6. 06

    Exit / Realisation

    We plan the exit with the same care as the entry, and pursue realisation at the point that best serves the investment.

Asset Management

Active management of assets and investment strategies with a disciplined, long-term approach.

Our approach

We manage assets with attention, patience and a firm view of risk.

Our role is to protect and grow the capital entrusted to us, through active oversight and decisions grounded in a long-term perspective rather than short-term movements.

Principles of management

Active portfolio oversight

Continuous review of every position, so that the portfolio reflects current conditions and stated objectives.

Risk management

Risk is identified, measured and weighed before and after every decision, and never treated as an afterthought.

Long-term value creation

Decisions are made with the full life of an investment in view, favouring durable value over short-term gains.

Capital preservation

Protecting capital is a starting point for every strategy, and a standard against which each decision is tested.

Strategic asset allocation

A considered distribution of capital across assets and strategies, aligned with each investor's objectives and horizon.

Investment Approach

Five principles guide how we select, structure and manage every investment.

Selectivity

We pursue few opportunities and say no often. Quality of opportunity matters more than the quantity of transactions.

Discipline

Clear criteria and consistent processes, applied with patience and without pressure to act.

Alignment

Our interests are structured to be aligned with those of our investors and partners, from the first discussion to the final realisation.

Transparency

Open, timely and honest communication with everyone who participates in an investment.

Long-term value creation

We invest with a long horizon, and focus on building value that lasts.
